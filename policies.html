Returns & refunds

We want you to love what you wear. In line with UAE consumer-protection law (Federal Law No. 15 of 2020 and its Executive Regulations), you may return an item bought online within 14 days of receiving it, for any reason.

To be eligible for a return, the item must be:

  • Unworn and unwashed, in its original condition;
  • With all tags attached and in its original packaging.

How it works

  • Free returns within the UAE. Email hello@sahraandbeyond.ae with your order number and we will arrange collection or a drop-off label — we cover return shipping inside the UAE.
  • Once we receive and inspect the return, we issue your refund within 14 days to your original payment method.
  • Exchanges (different size or fit) are free within the UAE, subject to stock.

Faulty, damaged or incorrect items

If your item arrives faulty, damaged or is not what you ordered, contact us within 14 days and we will replace it or refund you in full, including any delivery costs — as required by UAE law. This does not affect your statutory rights.

Exceptions

For hygiene and fairness, we cannot accept returns of items that have been worn, washed, altered or damaged after delivery, or items marked as final sale. Gift cards (if offered) are non-refundable.

What we collect

  • Email address: if you join our waitlist or newsletter, so we can tell you about drops and updates. You can unsubscribe any time.

Who processes your data

We use trusted providers to run the store: Shopify (checkout, orders and payments), Kit (email/newsletter), our payment gateway and courier (to take payment and deliver), and Google Analytics (anonymised site analytics). We never sell your data.

Cookies
