Size & fit

Find your fit

Every tee comes in two fits. Regular sits true to size and clean on the shoulder. Oversized is a relaxed, dropped-shoulder cut — size down if you want it less roomy. Heavyweight 100% organic cotton, pre-washed for minimal shrinkage.

Regular Fit

Classic, true-to-size cut. Straight body, set shoulder seam. Pick your usual size.

Oversized Fit

Relaxed drop-shoulder, longer body. Roomy by design — size down for a slightly trimmer look.
