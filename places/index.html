This is the map behind the brand: every desert, dune field, wadi, mountain trail and beach we have explored across the UAE. Each place has its own guide: how to get there, when to go, what it costs, the real risks, and a packing list matched to the trip, with the sources for every fact.

Dunes

Big RedSharjah · DunesBig Red is a 60–100m red sand dune on the Dubai–Hatta road at Al Madam, on the Sharjah side — the UAE's best-known spot for dune bashing, quad biking and sandboarding.The Empty Quarter (Rub' al Khali)Abu Dhabi · DunesThe vast sand desert of the southern Arabian Peninsula, shared by four countries, which the UAE reaches at its northern edge around Liwa in Abu Dhabi's Al Dhafra region.Half DesertDubai · DunesHalf Desert is a stretch of old road near Al Awir where drifting dunes have half-buried the tarmac — a favourite easy spot to learn desert driving or take a photo of sand reclaiming a road.Liwa OasisAbu Dhabi · DunesMoreeb Dune, a mega-dune usually quoted at around 300m on the edge of Liwa Oasis where the Rub' al Khali begins. Home to the annual dune-bashing festival, with date farms and forts strung along the oasis road behind it.MleihaSharjah · DunesAn archaeological desert in Sharjah where dunes meet Fossil Rock, Camel Rock and Bronze Age tombs — home to Mleiha National Park and the UNESCO-listed Faya Palaeolandscape.Moreeb Dune (Tal Moreeb)Abu Dhabi · DunesA very large sand dune south of the Liwa villages, usually quoted at around 300m high with a slipface of about 50 degrees. A paved road reaches its base; the sand itself is for 4x4s and the annual hill climb.

Camping

Al Quaa DesertAbu Dhabi · CampingOne of the darkest accessible skies in the Emirates. Deep in the southern desert, far from any city glow, it's a magnet for stargazers and astrophotographers chasing the Milky Way.Al Rams BeachRas Al Khaimah · CampingA quiet, causeway-linked beach at RAK's northern tip, on the Saraya Islands — calm, shallow water for kayaking and paddling, but a 4x4 with deflated tyres is needed to explore beyond the first stretch of sand.Crescent Moon LakeDubai · CampingA crescent-shaped artificial lake off the beaten track in Dubai's Al Qudra desert, known for oryx sightings at dawn. Reaching it needs a 4x4 — there's no paved road for the final stretch.Al Qudra Desert LakeDubai · CampingA small, undocumented lake inside Dubai's Al Qudra Lakes cluster, a couple of kilometres from Love Lake — quieter than the named lakes nearby, with no facilities at all.HattaDubai · CampingDubai's mountain exclave, cooler than the coast, in the Hajar range, on the Oman border. Kayak the turquoise Hatta Dam reservoir, ride graded trails at Hatta Wadi Hub, and walk the restored Heritage Village.Love LakeDubai · CampingTwo heart-shaped artificial lakes in Dubai's Al Marmoom reserve, reached by paved road. A free photo, picnic and camping spot with resident birdlife.Wadi KubRas Al Khaimah / Fujairah · CampingA quiet wild-camping wadi in the Hajar Mountains near Masafi, reached off the Gragrah–Ghub road, with century-old Sidr trees, rock pools after rain and no facilities at all.Zakher LakeAbu Dhabi · CampingA man-made desert lake on the edge of Al Ain, formed by rising groundwater from treated wastewater — now a magnet for flamingos and migratory birds. Swimming isn't permitted.

Wadis

Wadi ShawkaRas Al Khaimah · WadisA family-friendly Hajar Mountains wadi in Ras Al Khaimah with a free dam park, seasonal pools after rain and marked trails, plus longer routes for serious hikers.Wadi WurayahFujairah · WadisFujairah's UNESCO-listed mountain reserve in the Hajar range, home to what The National and Khaleej Times call the country's only permanent natural waterfall. It is open to visitors only on guided tours.

Mountains

Al Rafisah DamSharjah · MountainsAn emerald reservoir in the Hajar Mountains above Khor Fakkan, with a landscaped lakeside park, hiking trails, kayaking and a 55m man-made waterfall on Sharjah's east coast.Hajar MountainsRas Al Khaimah, Fujairah, Sharjah & Dubai (Hatta) · MountainsA range about 700km long that runs from the northern UAE into Oman. From the UAE side the usual ways in are Jebel Jais, Wadi Naqab and Jebel Yanas in Ras Al Khaimah, the wadis of Fujairah and Hatta, with Jebel Hafeet standing apart near Al Ain.Jebel YanasRas Al Khaimah · MountainsA rugged Ras Al Khaimah summit reached via the Wadi Naqab road — sunset ridge views, an old mountain village and a checkpoint you pass free of charge.Jebel HafeetAbu Dhabi · MountainsThe only mountain in Abu Dhabi emirate, about 1,249m, straddling the Oman border above Al Ain. A paved road climbs 1,200m through 60 hairpin bends to a summit hotel, with hot springs and Bronze Age tombs at the base.Jebel JaisRas Al Khaimah · MountainsThe UAE's highest mountain massif in the Hajar range: a paved road to a viewing deck at 1,250m, the Jais Flight zipline, mountain camps and air usually about 10°C cooler than the coast.Wadi NaqabRas Al Khaimah · MountainsA dramatic Hajar Mountains wadi in Ras Al Khaimah with red-rock scrambles, seasonal pools and a small dam — a genuine mountain hike with real flash-flood risk after rain.

Coast

Al Jazirah Al HamraRas Al Khaimah · CoastAn abandoned coral-stone pearling and fishing town on the Ras Al Khaimah coast, now run as a heritage village with a fort, souq, mosque and restored courtyard houses. It is on UNESCO's tentative list.Dibba RockFujairah · CoastA rocky outcrop off Fujairah's Dibba coast, reached by a short boat ride, with clear water, coral and turtles — a reliable East Coast snorkel and dive site, best October to April.Khor Fakkan BeachSharjah · CoastA free public crescent of sand on the Gulf of Oman in Sharjah's east-coast enclave, with a redeveloped corniche, playgrounds, boat trips to Shark Island and a lit waterfall beside it.Ras Al Khor Wildlife SanctuaryDubai · CoastA protected Ramsar wetland at the head of Dubai Creek with three bird hides, flamingos reported all year and large winter bird numbers. A Dh650-million redevelopment has been reported to have closed it temporarily, so check first.Shuweihat IslandAbu Dhabi · CoastAbu Dhabi's remote western coast holds Mars-like, mushroom-shaped rocks at Shuweihat Island — reachable by causeway, but public access is genuinely disputed near a military airfield.Sir Bani Yas IslandAbu Dhabi · CoastA natural island off Abu Dhabi reached by ferry from Jebel Dhanna: free-roaming oryx, giraffe and cheetah, a 7th-century monastery, and safaris you book through the resorts.Snoopy IslandFujairah · CoastA small rock islet off Al Aqah beach in Fujairah, reached by swimming or paddling out — turtles, reef sharks and reef fish make it a well-known UAE shore snorkel, best Oct–May.

Heritage

Falaj Al MuallaUmm Al Quwain · HeritageA historic oasis in Umm Al Quwain, its 1825 fort and scattered watchtowers rising from date-palm groves and open desert — a real, low-key stop on a UAE heritage or desert drive.
