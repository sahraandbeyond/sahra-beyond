Before you buy

Questions before you buy

Do you deliver across the UAE?

Yes. Delivery in the UAE is free and next-day when you order by 2 pm. GCC orders arrive in 3 to 5 days, free over AED 390, and worldwide orders in 7 to 14 days.

Who are the tees cut for, and what fits are there?

The Founding Edition and Sahel tees are cut unisex, in sizes S to XL, and each design comes in Regular and Oversized fits. The Sahel hoodies are unisex too. The Sahra Polo, the Sahel Polo and the Sahra Trail running pieces are cut for men. The size guide has the measurements.

What are the tees made of?

Heavyweight combed, ring-spun cotton, pre-washed. The Sahra Polo is cotton piqué. Collar labels are printed, so there is no tag at the neck. More on our fabric.

Can I exchange?

Yes, within the UAE. A different size or fit is free to exchange within 14 days of delivery, subject to stock, if the piece is unworn and unwashed with its tags on. The full terms are in returns and exchanges.

Can I pay in instalments?

Yes. On orders in AED, you can split the total into 4 payments with Tabby at checkout.
