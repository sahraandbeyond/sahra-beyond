Our story

It started with a camping trip in the desert

One night under a sky thick with stars was all it took. That trip lit a spark — a passion to explore more of this landscape that only grew with every journey after.

Years of exploring the farthest corners of the UAE became a way of seeing it. Sahra & Beyond turns those places into things you can wear.

Our commitment

A share of every sale plants ghaf trees

The ghaf is the UAE's national tree — indigenous, drought-hardy, and vital to the desert every one of our designs is drawn from. So a share of every sale goes to planting more of them, through Give a Ghaf — Goumbook's UAE native-tree programme. Wear a place; help it grow back.
