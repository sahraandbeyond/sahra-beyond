How these are measured
  • Chest — garment laid flat, measured straight across from armhole seam to armhole seam (pit‑to‑pit), 1″ below the armhole.
  • Length — from the highest point of the shoulder, at the collar seam, straight down to the bottom hem.
  • Shoulder — seam to seam across the back, between the shoulder seam points.
  • Sleeve — from the shoulder seam to the sleeve opening edge.

Tolerance is ±0.5″ (±1.3 cm) on every measurement. Garments outside that are rejected at QC.

05 — Care

Keeping the print sharp

Nothing unusual — but these five habits are what keep a DTG print crisp and the cotton soft.

  • Machine wash cold, inside out — heat is what fades a print fastest.
  • Wash with similar colours, on a gentle cycle.
  • Hang to dry. Tumble drying is the main cause of shrinkage and cracking.
  • Do not iron directly on the print — iron inside out if you need to.
  • Skip bleach and fabric softener; softener coats the fibres and dulls the finish.

07 — Questions

Before you order

Is the print going to crack?

DTG ink bonds into the cotton rather than sitting on top as a thick layer, so it flexes with the fabric instead of cracking. Washing cold and hanging to dry — rather than tumble drying — is what makes the difference long term.

Are these unisex? Will they fit women?

Yes — every piece we make is cut unisex, in one run of sizes from S to XL. There is no separate men's or women's version and no difference in the cut. Because the size chart lists the garment measured flat rather than a body size, the reliable way to choose is to measure a t-shirt you already like the fit of and match the numbers. Plenty of people size down for a closer fit or up for a relaxed one — both are the same garment.

Will it shrink?

The fabric is pre-washed to minimise shrinkage. Wash cold and hang dry and you should see very little movement. Hot washes and tumble dryers are what cause shrinkage.

Is 230gsm heavy for UAE weather?

It's a heavyweight tee, so it's more substantial than a thin fast-fashion shirt — that's what gives it structure and longevity. It's 100% cotton and the DTG print keeps the fabric breathable rather than sealing it, so it stays wearable. For peak summer, the Regular fit breathes more than the Oversized.

What if I order the wrong size?

Exchanges within the UAE are free, subject to stock, within 14 days of delivery — as long as it's unworn, unwashed and still has tags. Email hello@sahraandbeyond.ae with your order number and we'll arrange it.

Will this design be restocked?

This is a limited first run. We may make more of a design later, but we don't promise it — when a size sells out in this run, treat it as gone.
